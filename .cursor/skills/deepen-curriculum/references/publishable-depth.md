# Publishable depth — remediation contract

Map-complete means the planned files exist and module metadata validates. It says nothing about whether a learner can build a causal model, diagnose a failure, or transfer the method.

Publishable means a named independent reviewer has verified all of the following for this module:

1. The module opens with a precise this-system invariant or question and carries it through the seven-step loop.
2. A coverage contract maps every outcome to explanation, worked reasoning, practice, assessment evidence, and a materially changed transfer task.
3. Attacker capabilities, authority, trust boundaries, relevant state, and time horizon constrain each important claim.
4. Root cause, preconditions, impact, prevention, detection, and recovery are distinguished. Controls are derived from the property rather than introduced as a shopping list.
5. Mechanism limits, bypass conditions, framework defaults, residual risk, and review triggers are explicit.
6. Current standards are pinned to canonical sources with version, status, and exact identifiers or exact named principles. Awareness lists do not define the syllabus.
7. The lab is authorized, local, synthetic, isolated, resettable, and module-specific. Its vulnerable form fails for the intended forbidden outcome and its fixed form passes because of the taught structural change.
8. The assessment samples explanation, design, build, break, verify, operate, and communication without exposing keys. Critical security gaps cannot be hidden by an average.
9. Guided practice has observable success criteria and fades toward independent work.
10. The transfer task changes an asset, actor, authority relation, boundary, state transition, or time horizon—not merely product nouns.
11. Human-in-the-loop controls include usable and accessible failure/recovery paths where relevant.
12. A dated review artifact records semantic scores, exact lab commands/results, blockers, reviewer identity, and independence.

## Revision order

Run standards-pin, revise the spec and coverage contract, author lessons, author the lab, author assessment and isolated keys, update the SecureCollab spiral artifact, execute tests, then run quality-gate and lab-safety review. Update STATUS last.

A generator may create a draft scaffold. It must clear prior review metadata and cannot set publishable depth, competent quality, reviewer identity, or a review date.
