# Operate the scope rule and review a changed case

**Kind:** transfer-challenge
**Loop step:** 6 Operate · 7 Generalize · Review

When a denied host is requested, record the hostname and `reason=out_of_scope`; do not store response bodies or screenshots. Stop, tell the instructor or owner, and do not continue testing.

Transfer the rule to a contractor asked to test a customer's WordPress site. What written artifact, named host, and stop condition would you require? A cloud Juice Shop you do not own stays out of scope even when the course mentions Juice Shop.

## Review checklist

Reject a change that:

- treats TCP reachability, a login page, or a job title as authorization;
- follows a redirect outside the written list;
- logs public response bodies;
- claims a green scanner or WSTG chapter proves permission.

**Ready to continue:** you have a local scope note, a vulnerable-fail/fixed-pass result, a privacy-safe deny record, and a transfer answer. Next, module 0.2 checks which tooling bridges you need; it does not grant security clearance.
