**Kind:** operations-exercise
**Loop step:** 6 Operate

Store the evidence summary and bridge ids, then audit that 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, and Gate 1 remain on the learner's required path. Record `phase1_skip_denied` when a score-based skip is requested. Never log quiz answers or use a badge as recovery evidence.
