# 0.2 assessment (learner-facing — no answers)

**Practical evidence, not a compensating average.** States: not attempted | developing | competent | transfer-ready. Every critical invariant below needs satisfactory evidence on its own; a strong answer on one claim never substitutes for a missing one on another.

## Module

Diagnostic and adaptive bridge — five teaching claims (C1–C5), named in `spec.md` §Teaching claims: a score cannot satisfy Gate 1 evidence, a credential cannot substitute for diagnostic evidence, modules 1.2/1.3/1.4 are a fixed required set, the NICE work role is vocabulary rather than a syllabus, and a denial signal must not leak the thing it denies.

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Evidence-source table (Lesson 02) distinguishing what the diagnostic itself observed from what was merely asserted by a credential
- [ ] Local reproduction of this module's three forbidden outcomes (Lesson 03): a score-based skip, a credential overriding real diagnostic evidence, and a score or flag dropping module 1.4 from the required set
- [ ] Lab `labs/0.2/0.2-bridge`: `vulnerable/` tests show 8 of 15 failing for the stated reasons; `fixed/` tests show 15 of 15 passing
- [ ] Assessment items (`content/modules/0/0.2/assessment/items.md`) attempted with written reasoning, not single-word answers
- [ ] Seeded review (Lesson 08) completed via the four-question checklist, including correctly clearing the one non-issue line — do not open the key first
- [ ] Operate signal (Lesson 06) for a denied skip attempt that names its basis (score, credential, or required-set) and carries no quiz item text, answer content, or badge image
- [ ] Transfer task (Lesson 07) naming which of C1–C4 transfer unchanged to a clinic's hiring diagnostic and which needs its cited standard swapped

## Rubric

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Developing | Names C1 alone ("a score isn't authorization") without C2–C5; a job title, vendor certification, or LMS badge accepted as evidence in a written answer without challenge; missing forbidden-outcome lab evidence for any of C1–C3 |
| Competent | All five claims stated as system-specific properties, not slogans; all three lab forbidden outcomes reproduced and mapped to the claim each tests; the observed-versus-asserted distinction from Lesson 02 applied correctly, including to the seeded review's non-issue line |
| Transfer-ready | Lesson 07's transfer task completed, correctly identifying which claims transfer unchanged and which claim's standard reference must be swapped, without a job title, certification, or "they're clearly senior" standing in for any of C1–C4 |
