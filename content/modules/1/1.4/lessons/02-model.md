# 1.4-LO-02 — Risk register (not a CVE heatmap)

**Kind:** design-exercise  
**Loop step:** 2 Model  

## Property (start here)

Can a reviewer see **assumption, uncertainty, user-harm, residual, owner** per row?

NIST 800-63-4 (final) is not a password-complexity checklist here — it is CX as a security outcome.
