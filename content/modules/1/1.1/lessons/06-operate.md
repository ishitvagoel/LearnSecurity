# Operate when a claim fails

**Kind:** operations-exercise
**Loop step:** 6 Operate

## Prevention is not the whole response

For one catalogue row, describe a privacy-safe signal, an owner, containment, root-cause repair, recovery validation, and communication. Do not log note bodies, passwords, tokens, or unnecessary personal attributes.

Example for a false authorization claim:

- **Signal:** repeated cross-company deny decisions, with route and policy version only.
- **Owner:** the service owner pauses the affected release.
- **Containment:** disable the affected operation or revoke its grant path.
- **Repair:** fix the enforcement point and every alternate path to the same effect.
- **Recovery check:** rerun normal, negative, abuse, and failure evidence.
- **Communication:** tell affected owners what was exposed and what remains uncertain.

A claim about recovery must include accessible, understandable status and a safe failure path.
