# 1.1-LO-07 — Transfer: unfamiliar product card

**Kind:** transfer-challenge  
**Loop step:** 7 Generalize  
**Gate 1:** define security without naming a tool or vulnerability.

## Property

The method must work when the product is **not** SecureCollab.

## Prompt (no scaffold)

A municipal **appointment booking** site: residents book clinic slots; staff confirm; SMS reminders; a vendor calendar iframe.

Write a short invariant catalogue (five properties minimum) with attacker, trust, non-goals, and one residual. **Do not** mention OWASP Top 10, CVE, or a scanner. **Do not** target any real city website.

## Success

A reviewer can test or reject each line. You explain which SecureCollab lines **do not transfer** and why.
