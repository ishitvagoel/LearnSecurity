## Decision

**PASS — the PR #14 evidence-correlation finding is closed.**

The reviewers confirmed that:

- each server-held grant has a separate synthetic `audit_ref` used only as a
  non-bearer `capability_ref` in evidence;
- `worker_export` continues to accept only the grant ID, and presenting
  `capability-ref-01` as authority returns `unknown_grant` with empty output;
- `_next_correlation_id()` creates a distinct process-local identifier inside the
  evidence producer;
- requester-supplied `X-Correlation-ID: attacker-chosen` cannot control or enter
  emitted evidence;
- worker decision events carry the safe capability reference, while public events
  carry `None` rather than attacker-selected grant metadata;
- events exclude raw grant IDs, note bodies, bearer/header values, placeholder
  tokens, and requester-selected correlation values; and
- documentation states that these synthetic values do not prove global
  uniqueness, durable lifecycle linkage, concurrency safety, or tamper-resistant
  audit storage.
