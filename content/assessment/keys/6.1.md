## Property

Structural APIs (argv list, parameterized SQL in 5.5) are the mechanism; denylists of metacharacters are incomplete. ASVS anchors: `v5.0.0-1.2.5`, `v5.0.0-1.2.4` (same shape); `v5.0.0-1.2.10` is **Level 3, advanced**. Top 10 A05 and CWE-77/78/89 are awareness after the cause.
