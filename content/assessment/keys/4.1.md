# Examiner notes — 4.1 (not learner-facing)

## Property

After an account is deleted, that subject’s leftover session must not authenticate. Lifecycle is complete mediation across account states, not a login screen. Disable-login and SSO brand names are not revocation.

ASVS anchors: `v5.0.0-7.4.2`, `v5.0.0-7.4.1`; `v5.0.0-6.5.6` is **Level 3, advanced**. NIST SP 800-63-4 (final).
