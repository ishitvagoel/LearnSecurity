# Examiner notes — 6.3 (not learner-facing)

## Property

A state-changing share POST from a foreign origin without a matching CSRF token/origin check is denied. Ambient cookies are not consent.

## Lab

- `labs/6.3/6.3-lab/vulnerable/` must fail: Cross-origin state-changing POST authorized by cookie alone
- `labs/6.3/6.3-lab/fixed/` must pass.
- Root cause: Cookie authority used without site-bound intent.

## Seeded review intended findings

- Cookie auth + no Origin check
- GET /share?to= 
- CORS * with credentials
- Token in cookie not bound to session

## Transfer

Reject answers that name Top 10 / CWE Top 25 items as the definition of security.
Require a system-specific invariant, attacker, trust, residual risk.
Acceptable transfer direction: postMessage, clickjacking, CORS * with credentials.

## Operate

Accept a detect/recover note that does not log bodies/secrets. Example signal: foreign_origin_post_denied.
