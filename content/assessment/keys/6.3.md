# Examiner notes — 6.3 (not learner-facing)

## Property

A state-changing share POST from a foreign origin without a matching CSRF token/origin check is denied. Ambient cookies are not consent. ASVS anchors: `v5.0.0-3.5.1`, `v5.0.0-3.5.3`, `v5.0.0-3.3.2`; `v5.0.0-3.5.8` is **Level 3, advanced**. SameSite is a helper.

## Lab

- `labs/6.3/6.3-lab/vulnerable/` must fail: `test_foreign_origin_post_is_denied` and `test_same_origin_without_token_is_denied`
- Honest-path `test_same_origin_with_token_is_allowed` and `test_missing_cookie_is_denied` may pass on both
- `labs/6.3/6.3-lab/fixed/` must pass all
- Root cause: cookie authority used without site-bound intent

## Seeded review intended findings

- Cookie auth + no Origin check
- GET `/share?to=`
- CORS `*` with credentials
- Token in a cookie not bound to the session

## Transfer

Reject Top 10 / CWE as the definition. Accept clinic share-with-partner POST; postMessage, clickjacking, CORS `*` with credentials as residuals.

## Operate

Accept a detect/recover note that does not log cookies. Example: `foreign_origin_post_denied`.
