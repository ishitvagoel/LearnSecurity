# Examiner notes — 4.2 (not learner-facing)

## Property

A password or OTP at a lookalike origin is not phishing-resistant. WebAuthn at evil.example must fail. Passwords at the real origin remain phishable — do not advertise them as resistant. WebAuthn L3 is a **Candidate Recommendation**. ASVS `v5.0.0-6.3.3` Level 2 MFA; hardware phishing-resistant clause is **Level 3, advanced**.

## Lab

- `labs/4.2/4.2-lab/vulnerable/` must fail `test_password_is_not_phishing_resistant`, `test_otp_is_not_phishing_resistant`, and `test_webauthn_wrong_origin_fails`
- `labs/4.2/4.2-lab/fixed/` must pass, including matching-origin WebAuthn
- Root cause: shared secret replayable at the wrong origin

## Seeded review intended findings

- phishing_resistant('password', evil, real) True
- Marketing copy “MFA = phishing resistant”
- Recovery SMS as default
- No wrong-origin WebAuthn test

## Transfer

Reject Top 10 / CWE as the definition of security.
Acceptable direction: clinic staff SSO; step-up for export still origin-bound.
WCAG 2.2 required on the human ceremony.

## Operate
