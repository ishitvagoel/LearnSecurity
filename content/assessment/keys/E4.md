# Examiner notes — E4 (not learner-facing)

## Lab

- `labs/E4/e4-lab/vulnerable/` must fail the forbidden-outcome test.
- `labs/E4/e4-lab/fixed/` must pass.

## Seeded review intended findings

1. Mechanism named as if it were the property (false assurance).
2. Missing attacker capabilities or trust assumptions.
3. Happy-path success treated as evidence the invariant holds.
4. Scanner-green or awareness-list coverage treated as compliance.

## Transfer

Reject answers that name Top 10 / CWE Top 25 items as the definition of security. Require a system-specific invariant, attacker, trust, and residual risk.
