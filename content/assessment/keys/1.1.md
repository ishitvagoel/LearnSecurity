## SecureCollab catalogue anchors

Do not require the exact wording of the fixed fixture. A competent pack should cover at least five distinct property shapes and should usually include:

- cross-tenant note disclosure through more than one channel;
- authorized membership or note-state transitions, including denial/retry behavior;
- privacy-safe evidence for a high-impact action;
- bounded availability or recovery under shared-resource abuse/failure;
- privacy or safety that constrains logging, retention, export, deletion, or recovery.

Each row needs a complete claim envelope. Reject rows that merely fill fields with generic text.

## Causal-reasoning anchors

For a mechanism-only claim:

- **root cause:** property and mechanism were collapsed, often with hidden trust or scope;
- **preconditions:** the mechanism exists and the reviewer accepts its label as outcome evidence;
- **impact:** unrelated security decisions can be marked complete and forbidden outcomes remain unspecified;
- **prevention:** bound the property and derive a mechanism with proof obligations;
- **detection:** semantic review and property-focused negative/abuse/failure evidence;
- **recovery:** reopen decisions that relied on the claim, repair root cause, revise tests, and repeat independent review.

Do not require these exact words; require causal separation.

## Evidence anchors

A property oracle observes the forbidden outcome or protected state. Control-presence evidence observes a mechanism, configuration, call, badge, or status. A competent answer explains that both may be useful but support different conclusions.

Four evidence modes must be materially different:

- normal: permitted behavior and resulting state/evidence;
- negative: denied behavior and absence of partial effects;
- abuse: adversarial variation in identity, object, order, volume, or context;
- failure: dependency, retry, restore, evidence, or timeout behavior.

## Operations anchors

Accept different event schemas if they are privacy-safe and sufficient. Reject note bodies, passwords, tokens, raw authorization headers, or unnecessary contact attributes. Require evidence-pipeline failure behavior, bounded containment authority, root-cause repair, recovery validation, communication, and at least one operator-compromise residual risk.

Where a person acts, require a clear completion state, accessible path or alternative, and safe failure behavior.

## Knowledge-check key

1. Password hashing may support resistance to plaintext recovery from a verifier-store compromise under stated assumptions; it does not mediate subject-object note access.
2. A time horizon identifies whether the claim covers only a live request or also retained logs, exports, deletion, backup, restore, and incident periods.
3. Mechanism evidence: a cookie flag or middleware configuration exists. Property evidence: an unauthorized cross-tenant request yields no protected data or partial state across in-scope routes.
4. Accountability needs evidence; privacy limits content, access, purpose, and retention of that evidence.
5. Re-model service identity, authority, queue state, retries, trust boundary, evidence path, failure behavior, and time.

Equivalent bounded answers are acceptable.

## Transfer-ready indicators

CivicClinic must not be SecureCollab with renamed nouns. Look for reasoning about:

- delegated guardian authority and revocation;
- appointment existence and accommodation metadata as sensitive assets;
- shared-phone and changed-number assumptions;
- SMS and calendar-vendor boundaries;

- time-dependent booking/rescheduling/cancellation state;

Require at least three explicit SecureCollab claims or assumptions that fail to transfer and why. A non-obvious property conflict and narrowed universal claim are strong evidence. Tool lists, real-service research, or generic best practices are developing.

## Common blockers

- fewer than five meaningful catalogue rows;
- generic attacker or “backend trusted” language;
- mechanisms embedded as properties;
- only happy-path evidence;
- validator pass presented as application assurance;
- no mechanism limits or residual risk;
- unsafe target or data scope;
- transfer case changes only nouns;
- generated reviewer metadata offered as independent review.
