# Examiner notes — 5.2 (not learner-facing)

## Property

protect(secret) must provide authenticated confidentiality for the synthetic stored value: it must not be reversible as Base64, repeated plaintext must receive fresh nonces, and tampering must be rejected. Encoding is not confidentiality. ASVS anchors: `v5.0.0-11.2.1`, `v5.0.0-11.3.2`, `v5.0.0-11.3.3`; `v5.0.0-11.3.4` and `v5.0.0-11.1.4` are **Level 3, advanced**. RFC 9106 is for passwords, not this field. The fixed lab uses AES-GCM from `cryptography` with a process-local synthetic key; it does not prove key storage or rotation.
